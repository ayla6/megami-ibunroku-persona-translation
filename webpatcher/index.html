This is a port of the PSP translation in everywhere it can reasonably be! With the lines that were missing being mostly translated by Rei.

It's now called a beta because I'm really not sure when all of the bugs are going to be fixed and I really need you to know you have to update your game.

And remember that save states aren't compatible across even different patches much less different versions of the patches! Always load from the memory card when switching your version!

Source disc

It needs to a .bin or, just for the PPF patching, .chd (LZMA) of Megami Ibunroku Persona, not an iso or Revelations. Rev 0 and Rev 1 both work (we downgrade Rev 1).
